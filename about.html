Why this site exists

I spent eight years running working-memory and attention experiments at a behavioral neuroscience lab before going independent. The papers were dense - the takeaways were not.

MindFrame translates serious psychology and brain research into things you can actually do. Evidence first, no clickbait, no neuro-pop nonsense.
